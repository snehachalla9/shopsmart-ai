SHOPSMART AI - STORE POLICIES

1. RETURN POLICY
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving a product.
The product must be unused and returned with its original packaging.
Damaged or defective products can be reported to customer support.

2. REFUND POLICY
Refunds are initiated after the returned product passes inspection.
Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
The refund is sent to the original payment method.

3. SHIPPING POLICY
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.
Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location.
Customers receive order updates when their order is shipped.

4. CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers can request cancellation while the order is in Processing status.
Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.
Customers should contact support for assistance.

5. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Customers can contact ShopSmart support for help with returns, refunds, shipping, and order issues.
